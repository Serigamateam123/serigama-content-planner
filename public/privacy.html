Privacy Policy

Serigama Content Planner & Reporting · Last updated 31 July 2026

Serigama Content Planner & Reporting (“the app”) is a private, internal tool used by Serigama to plan its own social media content and view the performance of its own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Who this is for

The app is operated by Serigama for its own use. It is not a public service and does not serve or collect data from third parties.

What data we access

When you connect a TikTok account you own, the app uses TikTok’s official Login Kit and Display API to read, for that account only:

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar)
  • Account statistics (follower count, likes count, video count)
  • Your public videos and their public metrics (views, likes, comments, shares)

The app does not post on your behalf, does not read private messages, and does not access any account you have not explicitly connected.

How we store and use it

Data is stored in a private Supabase database used solely to display your content plan and analytics inside the app. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored securely and used only to refresh your own metrics.

Data retention & deletion

You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time, which stops further data collection. To have stored data deleted, contact us at the email below.
